Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a finished macOS desktop application icon for Copy Stack, a refined clipboard history utility. Single icon, not a presentation or contact sheet.
Primary request: design a completely new icon that embodies copying and an organized stack, matching an existing native-feeling interface of system blue, pearl white, warm gray, softly rounded surfaces and restrained glass.
Composition: square 1024x1024 canvas. One continuous-curvature macOS rounded-square pearl-white tile, about 84% of canvas width, centered with ample fully transparent margin beyond its rounded boundary. On the tile, create a bold, optically centered stack of THREE compact upright rounded clipboard/history cards, staggered slightly from upper-left at the back toward lower-right at the front. The grouped glyph occupies about 66% of tile width and height. Almost straight-on view, shallow physical depth, no dramatic perspective. Exactly three readable nested silhouettes.
Design: back card is muted pale ice-blue translucent glass; middle card is medium clear blue glass; front card is solid rich system blue (#0068df to #1870d8). The foremost card has one simple broad pill-shaped pearly clipboard clip centered on its top edge, and just TWO short thick softly rounded white horizontal bars in its upper-middle area, top bar longer, lower bar shorter. All details generously sized to read at 32px. The front card is a simple elegant solid silhouette; the stack is the identifying gesture. Balanced negative space, impeccably precise rounded edges.
Materials: satin pearl ceramic base (#f3f3f5 and #ecebea), very shallow bevel and faint light gray edge, crisp but subtle glass rim on the rear layers, satin blue enamel front layer, soft restrained contact shadows between cards. Soft studio light from upper left. Tactile and premium, calm and useful, contemporary macOS visual character without copying an existing app icon.
Constraints: genuinely transparent pixels outside the outer squircle; the icon itself is opaque. No text, letters, watermark, wordmark, devices, mockup scene, border frame, orange, coral, purple, neon glow, sparkles, iridescence, heavy lens effects, or extraneous badges. Do not make the backing tile circular. Output only the single production-quality icon.
