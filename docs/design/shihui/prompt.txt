Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a completely new finished macOS app icon for a clipboard recall tool named 拾回 (Shihui). Single icon only, no lettering or presentation board.
Creative direction: radically minimal, calm, precise, intelligent native Mac utility. Flat graphic design with just the tiniest satin depth. A strong unique symbol, not a literal clipboard.
Composition: 1024x1024 square canvas; a centered deep warm graphite continuous-curvature rounded-square tile, occupying 84% of canvas. Fully transparent exterior and ample equal clear margins. The graphite tile is almost matte (#292726 to #202023), uniform and clean, with a restrained smooth edge and absolutely no rough texture, grain, detached pixels or exterior glow.
Central symbol: ONE bold continuous ivory ribbon (#f3f3f5), equal stroke width, shaped into a generous open rounded-square inward-turning loop. Think a refined geometric path that turns back into itself, recalling the Chinese idea 回 (return), but NOT literal typography. The open square spiral should have no more than one and a quarter turns, a big open negative-space center, and a visibly open path. The outer opening is at the lower right. The inside end of the same ribbon finishes as a short system-blue segment (#0068df), seamlessly part of the single path, not a detached dot or extra object. The symbol occupies roughly 56% of tile width, perfectly optically centered. Broad curves, exactly consistent stroke, rounded ends, generous gaps. Must be simple enough to read at 24px. Make the overall mark expressive and distinctive, not a generic refresh circle or sync arrow.
Style: premium Swiss graphic identity translated into a macOS dock icon. Mostly two-dimensional, satin finish only, crisp geometry, very subtle rim light at most. Strong ivory versus graphite contrast, small quiet blue accent. No physical props, no 3D toy, no glass, no chrome, no heavy shadow, no volumetric extrusion.
Constraints: exactly one tile and one continuous mark. No clipboards, no stacked cards, no paper sheets, no documents, no text, no letters, no logo wordmark, no arrowheads, no infinity sign, no rainbow, no purple, no sparkles, no abstract complex knot. True transparency outside the tile; clean smooth alpha silhouette. Output the isolated single app icon, not a scene.
